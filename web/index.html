You’re always
in control.

Review first. See the path, purpose, and cleanup impact.

Keep what matters. Protect locations from cleanup.

Use the right tools. Files go to Trash; simulator removal uses Apple’s tools with additional confirmation.

No forced exits. No automatic cleanup or emptying Trash.

Allocated size is not a promise of freed space. Trash, APFS shared blocks, and snapshots affect what’s actually reclaimed. Some archives and device data cannot be recreated.
