02 / A LITTLE CARE GOES A LONG WAY

A gentle tidy.
Or a deeper checkup.

Two clear choices. You decide how far to go.

THE SAFE DEFAULT01
✳

Standard Clean

A little everyday breathing room. Clear regenerable caches, old logs, and idle build data.

  • Archives and release symbols stay
  • Source working copies stay
  • Device and test state stay
Less clutter. Your important parts intact.
MORE CHOICE, WITH CARE02
⌕

Deep Clean

Take a closer look at archives, devices, dependencies, and older tools. Each choice is yours.

  • Keep the latest 1, 3, or 5 archive versions
  • Keep every build of retained versions
  • Or select all unprotected archives
Always reviewed. Never an automatic reset.
!

A little extra care for release symbols. Each released build needs its own matching dSYMs. XcodeFit checks UUIDs and shows symbol sizes; it does not assume you have a backup. Protected locations always stay.
